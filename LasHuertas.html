About Las Huertas

About Us

Welcome to Las Huertas Staycation — where every stay is designed to feel like home, but with a touch of luxury and personality. Our name "Las Huertas" reflects warmth, abundance, and the feeling of a private garden escape right in the heart of Antipolo.

What started as a passion for creating unique spaces has grown into a collection of 7 signature units, each with its own distinct mood and character. From the maximalist energy of CASA ALLEGRO to the serene simplicity of CASA SEMPLICITA, we've curated every detail to match your vibe.

Located just minutes from LRT2 Antipolo and SM Masinag, we offer the perfect balance of accessibility and tranquility.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are your check-in and check-out times?
Regular check-in is from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Early or late check-in may be available upon request, subject to additional fees.
How much is the damage deposit?
A deposit of ₱1,000 is required upon booking. This becomes non-refundable upon cancellation.
What's the maximum number of guests per unit?
Each unit has a maximum capacity of 4 guests. Extra charges apply for groups of 3 or more (₱250 per additional guest beyond 2).
Is there a noise curfew?
Yes, please keep noise levels down from 10:00 PM onwards to respect our neighbors and other guests.
Do you offer WiFi and parking?
Yes! All units come with high-speed WiFi. Parking is available on a first-come, first-served basis.
What's your cancellation policy?
Cancellations incur a ₱1,000 non-refundable fee. Please review our cancellation policy before booking.
